Recipients

Cookies and local storage

The site sets no cookies. Your language choice is kept in your browser (local storage) and is never sent. The site uses no audience measurement tool. If one is added, it will be configured so that no consent is required, under the conditions set by the CNIL, and this page will be updated.

Your rights

You may access, rectify and erase your data, restrict or object to its processing, and request its portability. Write to dedicated e-mail address; we answer within one month. If you believe your rights are not respected, you may lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr), or with the authority of your country.

Security

Exchanges with the site are encrypted (HTTPS). Access to data is limited to authorised staff.
