We want everyone to be able to understand Meridian and reach us, whatever their device.

Current status

This site has not yet been audited against RGAA, the French accessibility standard. On every release all pages are tested automatically with axe-core (WCAG 2.2 levels A and AA) on desktop and mobile: no issue detected as of the update date. Automated tests only cover part of the criteria.

What we apply

  • Compliant contrast, visible focus, click targets of at least 36 px.
  • Full keyboard navigation, including the demo and the form.
  • Animations turned off when you ask for reduced motion.
  • Each page declares its language; six languages available.

Report a problem

If some content is not accessible to you, write to us: we will send it in another form and fix the site.
