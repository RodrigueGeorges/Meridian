The first connector in the catalogue. The merchant keeps its Stripe account and contract; Meridian drives the terminal through the Stripe Terminal API, server-side.

What the connector does

  • Payment, cancellation, refund and receipt through the five Meridian calls.
  • Server-driven terminal control: no Stripe SDK to integrate in the till.
  • Idempotency at every step and status lookup at Stripe when a response is lost: zero double charges.

Countries and payment methods

Available countries and payment methods are those of the merchant’s Stripe account. The Meridian catalogue shows the local methods supported in each country.

Status
