The protocol

Concert, now known as Caisse-AP, is the protocol used in France for till software to drive a payment terminal: the till sends the amount, the terminal returns the result.

What Meridian does

Meridian exposes a Concert bridge on the store’s local network (TCP port 8888 by default). The till talks to it as it would to a terminal; each till number is mapped to a Meridian point of sale, which routes the payment to the PSP chosen by the merchant.

  • No change needed in the till software.
  • Every payment received by the bridge goes through the same engine: signed journal, idempotency, recovery, zero double charges.
  • The merchant can switch PSP without touching the till.

Status

Bridge available in the demo and tested on Caisse-AP frames. Certification is pending: we will only announce it once obtained.
