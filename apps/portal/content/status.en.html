Every PSP in the catalogue, measured on the real payments that go through Meridian. Figures are recomputed every 30 seconds.

The status shows with JavaScript enabled. It is also available as JSON at /v1/status.

How it is measured

  • Response: the PSP returned an outcome (authorised, declined or cancelled). A card decline is a response: the PSP is working.
  • No response: technical failure, or a switch to the backup PSP because the primary PSP could not be reached.
  • Average time: from payment creation to outcome, card presentation included.
  • No volume, amount or merchant is ever published. Below 20 payments in the period, nothing is shown.
