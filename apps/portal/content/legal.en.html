Nature of the service

Meridian is a technical provider that connects till software and kiosks to payment service providers (PSPs). Meridian holds no funds, signs no contracts with merchants and stores no full card numbers. Payments are processed by the PSPs chosen by merchants.

The public demo on this site runs on PSP simulators: no real payment is made.
