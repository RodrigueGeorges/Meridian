Cookies and local storage

The site sets no cookies. Your language choice is kept in your browser (local storage) and is never sent. Audience measurement uses no cookie and no identifier: it only counts pages and actions and cannot link two visits. If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control or “Do Not Track” signal, no measurement data is sent.
