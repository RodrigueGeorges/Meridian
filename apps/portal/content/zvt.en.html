The protocol

ZVT is the reference protocol in Germany, Austria and Switzerland for connecting a till to a payment terminal.

What Meridian does

Meridian exposes a ZVT bridge on the store’s local network (TCP port 20007 by default), mapped to a Meridian point of sale. The till does not need to know which PSP processes the payment.

Supported commands: Registration, Authorization, Reversal, Refund, Abort, End-of-Day, Log-Off, Status Enquiry, Diagnosis.

  • No change needed in the till software.
  • Every payment received by the bridge goes through the same engine: signed journal, idempotency, recovery, zero double charges.
  • The merchant can switch PSP without touching the till.
