The protocol

nexo Retailer is an international standard for messages between till and terminal (SaleToPOIRequest and SaleToPOIResponse), used across Europe.

What Meridian does

Meridian accepts nexo Retailer messages in JSON or XML on POST /v1/nexo. The POIID field identifies the Meridian point of sale.

Supported messages, in JSON and XML: Login, Logout, Payment, Reversal, TransactionStatus, Abort, Reconciliation.

  • No change needed in the till software.
  • Every payment received by the bridge goes through the same engine: signed journal, idempotency, recovery, zero double charges.
  • The merchant can switch PSP without touching the till.
